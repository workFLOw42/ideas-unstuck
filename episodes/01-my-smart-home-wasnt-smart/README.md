# Part 1 — My smart home wasn't smart

> *"That's not smart. That's homework."*

▶️ **Watch:** [Part 1 on YouTube](https://youtube.com/shorts/_x9Uto50TOY)

No code in this part — it's about the problem.

## What I had

- A few **smart light bulbs** with their own app and a remote. Nice colour effects — but honestly,
  the light switch on the wall was faster.
- **Wi-Fi speakers** with their own app.
- A **music subscription** with its own app.
- And every new gadget wanted **one more app**.

## Why that wasn't smart

Each device was clever on its own, but nothing worked **together** — and nobody in the family wanted to open
an app to turn on a light. Owning gadgets just to own them isn't smart. Every gadget needs a real reason,
and it has to add value for everyone.
